🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Using SQL

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales and customer data using SQL. The goal is to extract meaningful business insights related to customer behavior, product performance, sales revenue, order trends, payment methods, and profitability.

The project uses a relational database consisting of five interconnected tables and applies SQL queries to solve real-world business questions.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify top-performing and best-selling products.
- Analyze revenue across different product categories.
- Evaluate monthly sales and order trends.
- Segment customers based on spending.
- Analyze payment success and order cancellation rates.
- Identify profitable products and valuable customers.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- SQL – Database management and business analysis.
- MySQL – SQL environment (the script uses MySQL-style syntax).
- Relational Database – Structured data management.

🗂️ Database Structure

The project consists of five tables:

Table| Description
Customers| Customer details, demographics, location, and signup date
Products| Product names, categories, prices, and costs
Orders| Order dates, customer IDs, payment methods, and order status
OrderDetails| Product quantities, unit prices, and discounts
Payments| Payment dates, amounts, methods, and payment status

🔍 Business Questions & Analysis

The project covers the following business analysis areas:

1. Sales Performance Analysis

- Total revenue from non-cancelled orders
- Total quantity of products sold
- Average order value
- Revenue by product category
- Monthly order count
- Monthly revenue

2. Product Performance Analysis

- Best-selling products by quantity
- Revenue generated by each product
- Average product price by category
- Number of products in each category
- Product revenue ranking
- Top 3 products in each category
- Most profitable products

3. Customer Analysis

- Total number of customers
- Customers who placed orders
- Top customers by revenue
- Customer spending segmentation
- Customers who never placed an order
- Customers with the most orders
- Customer lifetime value
- High-value but infrequent customers

4. Payment & Order Analysis

- Most frequently used payment method
- Order cancellation rate
- Payment success rate
- Revenue by payment method

5. Advanced SQL Analysis

- Ranking products by revenue
- Customer segmentation using CASE
- Month-over-month revenue growth using LAG()
